Dear Me

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Dear Me ("the App") processes, why, and what rights you have over it. It's written to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA"), and applies regardless of where you use the App from.

The short version: everything you enter into Dear Me stays on your device. We (WebRulers, the company behind Dear Me) never receive, see, store, or sell any of it — there is no server for it to go to. The only exception is a feedback email you choose to send us yourself (see section 10).

1. Who we are

Dear Me is provided by WebRulers ("we", "us", "WebRulers") and built by Nisarg Patel and Parshv Shah. For the purposes of the DPDPA, WebRulers is the Data Fiduciary for any personal data processed through the design of the App — even though that processing happens entirely on your own device, under your control.

2. What personal data the App processes

When you use Dear Me, the following data may be entered and stored — only ever locally, on your device:

DataExamples
Cycle dataPeriod start/end dates, flow intensity
Daily logsSymptoms, mood, notes, daily flow intensity
ProfileAn optional display name you choose
PreferencesTheme (light/dark), language
RemindersWhich reminders are on, timing preferences
App lockWhether a PIN or biometric lock is enabled

We don't collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifiers unless you choose to type something like that into a notes field yourself. If you email us feedback, we receive what's in that email (see section 10).

3. Why we process this data

Solely to provide the App's features to you: logging your cycle and symptoms, showing predictions (next period, fertile window, ovulation), and sending the reminders you've configured. If you email us, we use that email only to reply to you and to improve the App. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond what you see happening in the App.

4. How your data is processed — and why there's no one else in the loop

Dear Me has no backend server, no user accounts, and makes no network requests of any kind. All data is stored in a local database on your device and a small local settings store. We verified this directly in the App's source code — there is no code path that sends your data anywhere.

Because of this:

  • We don't have your data. We can't see it, analyze it, or be asked for it by anyone, because it never reaches us. Feedback you email us is the one thing that does, and only when you send it.
  • We don't share it with any third party, advertiser, analytics provider, or anyone else — there's no channel for that to happen.
  • There's no cloud backup built into the App. If you want a copy off your device, use the export feature described below.

5. How your data is protected on your device

  • The App's data lives in storage that's private to the App on your device (standard OS app sandboxing).
  • If you enable the App's optional PIN lock, your PIN is hashed before it's ever stored or checked — we never store it in plain text.
  • If you enable biometric lock, it's backed by your device's own secure, hardware-backed keychain.
  • We do not currently apply additional encryption to the App's local database beyond your device's own security. We recommend keeping a screen lock enabled on your device for extra protection of sensitive data like this.

6. How long we keep your data

For as long as you keep the App installed and don't delete it. Your data is removed the moment you either use "Reset all data" in Settings → Privacy & Data, or uninstall the App.

7. Your rights, and how to use them today

Under the DPDPA and as a matter of how the App is built, you have the right to:

  • Access and review your data — it's always visible to you within the App itself.
  • Export a copy — Settings → Privacy & Data → Export lets you download your data as a CSV file at any time.
  • Correct or update your data — edit any entry directly in the App.
  • Erase your data — Settings → Privacy & Data → Reset all data permanently deletes everything stored by the App on your device.
  • Withdraw consent — since nothing is processed outside your device, withdrawing consent is as simple as deleting your data or uninstalling the App.

Because there's no account or server-side record of you, there's no separate "request" process needed — these actions are built directly into the App and take effect immediately.

8. Children's data

Dear Me does not currently verify a user's age, and does not knowingly target children. We do not track, profile, or advertise to any user, of any age — so the specific protections the DPDPA requires around behavioural tracking and targeted advertising for children are met by the App simply not doing those things for anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has used the App, you can delete all locally stored data at any time via Reset all data, with no need to contact us.

9. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications directly through your device's operating system. These are not push notifications from a server — they're generated and delivered entirely on-device, using the dates and settings you've configured.

10. Feedback emails

Settings → Send feedback opens your own email app with a message addressed to info@webrulers.in. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there, and sending feedback is entirely optional.

If you do send it, we receive what you write, your email address, and the basic details prefilled at the bottom of the message: the app version, your device's operating system and version, and the App's language. The App never adds your cycle, symptom, mood, or notes data to it. Only include health details if you choose to type them in yourself.

We use feedback emails only to reply to you and to improve the App, never for marketing, and don't share them with anyone else. We delete them once they're no longer needed for that. You can ask us to delete an email you've sent at any time by writing to the same address.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes — for example, if new data types are added. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and you'll be asked to review and accept the updated Policy before continuing to use the App.

12. Grievance redressal and contact
